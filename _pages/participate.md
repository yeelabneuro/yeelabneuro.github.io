The CAMP Lab is recruiting participants for research studies conducted by researchers in the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences at the University of Iowa. Our studies aim to improve our understanding of how we make choices and perform cognitive or physical tasks under stress and motivational incentives.

To participate in any of our studies, you must be **18–55 years of age**. Interested participants will complete a brief (~5 minute) pre-screening survey to determine eligibility. Participation is completely voluntary.

---

## Motivation and Effort Study (Behavior)

**Purpose:** To improve our understanding of how we make choices and perform cognitive or physical tasks under stress and motivational incentives.

**Participation:** Eligible participants will be asked to complete several online surveys and in-person visits at the University of Iowa. In-person visits involve measuring physiological measures (e.g., heart rate) and performing computerized tasks with different types of motivation.

**Location:** Psychological and Brain Sciences Building, University of Iowa

**Time Involved:** 1 to 3 hours

**Compensation:** \\$15 per hour or SONA credit

**Interested?** [Complete the pre-screening survey](https://uiowa.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7Ot98psfX2395I2)

## Brain Mechanisms of Motivation and Effort Study (Pharmacology)

**Purpose:** To improve our understanding of how the medication _escitalopram_, a drug used to treat depression and generalized anxiety disorder, influences how we make choices and perform cognitive or physical tasks under stress and motivational incentives.

**Participation:** Eligible participants will be asked to complete several online surveys and three in-person visits at the Psychological and Brain Sciences Building and the MRRF at the Pappajohn Biomedical Discovery Building. In-person visits involve measuring physiological measures (e.g., heart rate), conducting magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) inside a scanner, and performing computerized tasks under stress (e.g., electrical stimulation, sounds). Further, a 20-milligram dose of escitalopram (medication often used to treat depression and anxiety) and a sugar pill will be administered. Outside of in-person visits, heart rate and sleep scores will be measured through fitted Oura rings.

**Location:** Psychological and Brain Sciences Building and MRRF, Pappajohn Biomedical Discovery Building, University of Iowa

**Time Involved:** 3 in-person sessions, up to 13 hours total

- Session 1: up to 1 hour
- Session 2: up to 6 hours
- Session 3: up to 6 hours

**Compensation:** Up to \\$340 total

- \\$10 per hour for the screening session (up to \\$10 for Session 1)
- \\$25 per hour for the pharmacology and MRI sessions (\\$150 for Session 2 and \\$150 for Session 3)
- \\$30 bonus for completing all 3 sessions and returning the Oura ring

**Interested?** [Complete the pre-screening survey](https://uiowa.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_eaOa2VjSuqAUkYe)

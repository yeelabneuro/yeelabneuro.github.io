## Gina Mazurenko

Gina Mazurenko is a Lab Manager for the Computational Affective Motivational Psychiatry (CAMP) Lab. She is a recent graduate of UCLA with interests in psychology, neuroscience, and computational approaches to mental health. In the lab, she supports research on motivation, affect, decision-making, and their relevance to mental health. She is especially interested in how behavioral and computational methods, and how they explain individual differences.

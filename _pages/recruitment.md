The CAMP Lab is recruiting **undergraduate RAs, graduate students, and postdoctoral researchers**. We welcome candidates with backgrounds in cognitive or affective neuroscience, computational modeling, data science, computer science, or clinical science. The CAMP Lab is committed to **inclusive excellence and mutual respect** — we believe diverse perspectives make for better science.

---

**<i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Graduate students**

Applicants may apply through the [Psychological & Brain Sciences Graduate Program](https://psychology.uiowa.edu/graduate) or the [Neuroscience Graduate Program](https://neuroscience.grad.uiowa.edu). The deadline is Dec 1, 2026.

---

**<i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Postdoctoral researchers**

We are actively recruiting postdocs to join the lab! Apply [here](/postdoc_application.html) (view [job description](/assets/pdf/Yee_Lab_Postdoc_Job_Ad.pdf)).

---

**<i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Undergraduate research assistants**

Apply [here](/assets/html/undergrad_ra_application.html). Please make sure to include a CV/resume, academic transcript, description of your past research experience and career goals, and relevant technical background.

---

**<i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Lab manager / post-bacc**

We are not currently recruiting for this position.

## Olivia Bartuska

Olivia Bartuska is a Research Assistant at the Computational Affective Motivational Psychiatry (CAMP) Lab. She is a third-year undergraduate student at the University of Iowa studying Biomedical Engineering with a focus on Biomaterials and Biomechanics. She is interested in learning how to improve neuromodulation technologies to advance our understanding and treatment of neurological and psychiatric conditions.

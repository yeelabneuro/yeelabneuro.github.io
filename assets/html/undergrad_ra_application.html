Undergraduate Research Assistant Application

Thank you for your interest in joining our lab as an undergraduate research assistant! We are looking for undergraduates from all majors (e.g., Psychology, Neuroscience, Computer Science, Engineering) who are curious and excited about computational methods for investigating mental health. In this role, you will contribute to ongoing research projects through hands-on involvement in data collection, programming, and statistical and modeling analysis. We require good academic standing and expect a commitment of approximately 10–12 hours per week. This is an excellent opportunity to gain real research experience, develop technical and analytical skills, and work closely with graduate students and faculty in a collaborative lab environment. We review applications on a rolling basis and will be in touch if your background is a strong fit. To learn more about our work, visit yeelabneuro.com.
